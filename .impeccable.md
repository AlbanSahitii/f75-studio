## Design Context

### Users
Mac owners of an AULA F75 who have had no way to configure it without Windows. They open the app briefly to change the lighting, remap a key or switch a profile, then close it. The job is to change one setting confidently and see it take effect on the keyboard right away.

### Brand Personality
Calm, premium, precise. The app should feel like a first-party Mac accessory app, the way Logitech Options+ does. Emotional goal: confidence ("I know what will happen when I click this") and quiet delight when the keyboard responds.

### Aesthetic Direction
- **Reference:** Logitech Options+. The device is the hero and the centre of the home screen; you click a part of the device (a key, the lighting) to open its settings; changes apply immediately, with no Save button.
- **Theme:** follows macOS light/dark appearance. Native-feeling system typography (SF via `system-ui`), generous spacing, quiet surfaces.
- **Colour:** neutral greyscale UI. The **only** colour comes from the keyboard's live RGB state shown in the device render, so the lighting itself is the accent.
- **Anti-references:** the AULA vendor driver's cramped tabs and tiny controls; the generic AI/SaaS look (purple gradients, glassmorphism, card-everything dashboards); gamer neon (glows, angled shapes, aggressive type).

### Design Principles
1. **Device first.** Every screen starts from the keyboard render; settings belong to the part you clicked.
2. **Live, not saved.** Every change is applied to the keyboard and reflected in the render immediately. Undo replaces confirmation dialogs.
3. **Colour is content.** Chrome stays neutral so RGB previews read accurately in both themes.
4. **One task per view.** Progressive disclosure: common settings up front, advanced options one step away.
5. **Mac-native manners.** Respect system appearance, reduced motion, keyboard navigation and VoiceOver labels on every control.
